1. MULTI-LAYER MARGIN ANALYSIS (all years)
   a. Gross Margin     = Gross Profit / Revenue × 100
   b. Operating Margin = Operating Income / Revenue × 100
   c. EBITDA Margin    = EBITDA / Revenue × 100
   d. Net Margin       = Net Income / Revenue × 100
   e. Normalized Margin = Normalized Income / Revenue × 100
   — Margin waterfall analysis: where is profitability being lost or gained?
 
2. RETURN ON EQUITY (ROE)
   — ROE = Net Income / Average Total Common Equity × 100
   — Benchmark: >15% Strong | 10–15% Adequate | <10% Below average
   — Trend: improving, stable, or declining?
 
3. RETURN ON ASSETS (ROA)
   — ROA = Net Income / Average Total Assets × 100
   — Benchmark: >5% Strong | 2–5% Adequate | <2% Low efficiency
 
4. RETURN ON INVESTED CAPITAL (ROIC)
   — NOPAT = EBIT × (1 - Effective Tax Rate)
   — ROIC = NOPAT / Invested Capital × 100
   — Benchmark: >10% Value-creating | <WACC (~8%) Value-destroying
 
5. DUPONT DECOMPOSITION (most recent year)
   — ROE = Net Profit Margin × Asset Turnover × Equity Multiplier
     Net Profit Margin = Net Income / Revenue
     Asset Turnover    = Revenue / Average Total Assets
     Equity Multiplier = Average Total Assets / Average Total Equity
   — Identify primary ROE driver: margin, efficiency, or leverage?
 
6. OPERATING LEVERAGE
   — Operating Leverage = % Change in Operating Income / % Change in Revenue
   — >1.0 = costs scaling slower than revenue (positive)
 
7. R&D INTENSITY
   — R&D % of Revenue and R&D % of Gross Profit (each year)
   — Increasing = investing in future | Decreasing = potentially harvesting
 
8. RED FLAGS
   — Gross margin declining >200bps annually
   — Operating margin <5% in non-commodity business
   — ROE declining despite increasing leverage
   — ROIC <5% (capital destruction)
 
9. ANALYTICAL VERDICT
   — Professional profitability assessment with DuPont context.
   — Rating: EXCEPTIONAL / STRONG / ADEQUATE / BELOW AVERAGE / WEAK
