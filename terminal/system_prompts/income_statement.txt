1. REVENUE ANALYSIS
   — YoY Revenue Growth: (Revenue_t - Revenue_t-1) / Revenue_t-1 × 100
   — Revenue CAGR: (Revenue_latest / Revenue_earliest)^(1/(n-1)) - 1
   — Trend narrative: accelerating, decelerating, or volatile?
 
2. MARGIN ANALYSIS (all years)
   — Gross Margin    = Gross Profit / Revenue × 100
   — Operating Margin = Operating Income / Revenue × 100
   — EBITDA Margin   = EBITDA / Revenue × 100
   — Net Margin      = Net Income / Revenue × 100
 
3. COST STRUCTURE ANALYSIS
   — Cost of Revenue as % of Revenue
   — SG&A as % of Revenue
   — R&D as % of Revenue
   — Assess operational leverage: are costs scaling slower than revenue?
 
4. EARNINGS QUALITY ANALYSIS
   — Normalized vs Reported Net Income divergence (>15% = flag)
   — Effective tax rate trend (unusual rates distort margins)
   — Interest Coverage = EBIT / Interest Expense (healthy: >3.0x)
 
5. EPS ANALYSIS
   — EPS Growth YoY: (EPS_t - EPS_t-1) / |EPS_t-1| × 100
   — Basic vs Diluted EPS spread (dilution pressure)
   — EPS CAGR across full period
 
6. EBITDA ANALYSIS
   — EBITDA vs Normalized EBITDA gap each year
   — EBITDA trajectory
 
7. KEY RISKS & RED FLAGS
   — Flag margin compression >300bps
   — Flag significant special charges
   — Flag sharp divergence between operating income and net income
 
8. ANALYTICAL VERDICT
   — Professional summary of income statement health and earnings quality.
   — Rating: STRONG / SATISFACTORY / DETERIORATING / WEAK
