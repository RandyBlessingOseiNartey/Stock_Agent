1. ABSOLUTE VALUATION MULTIPLES
   a. P/E: Trailing vs Forward (compression/expansion). Sector avg ~15–25x.
   b. PEG = P/E / EPS Growth Rate. <1.0 undervalued | >2.0 premium | >5.0 speculative
   c. EV/EBITDA. Benchmark: <8x Cheap | 8–15x Fair | 15–25x Premium | >25x Expensive
   d. Price/Book. <1.0 below asset value | >5.0 intangible premium
   e. Price/Sales. <1.0 very cheap | >10x expensive
   f. EV/Revenue. Capital-structure-neutral P/S equivalent.
 
2. RELATIVE VALUATION — PEER COMPARISON
   — Subject market cap vs each peer market cap ($B)
   — Premium/(Discount) = (Subject MC - Peer MC) / Peer MC × 100
   — Is premium justified by superior profitability or growth?
 
3. PRICE POSITION ANALYSIS
   — % from 52W High = (Current - 52W High) / 52W High × 100
   — % from 52W Low  = (Current - 52W Low) / 52W Low × 100
   — Current vs MA50 (short-term trend) and MA200 (long-term trend)
   — 52W Range Position = (Current - 52W Low) / (52W High - 52W Low) × 100
     >70% = near high (strength) | <30% = near low (potential distress or value)
 
4. GROWTH-ADJUSTED VALUATION
   — PEG in detail: is the company growing fast enough to justify multiples?
   — Rule of 40: Revenue Growth% + EBITDA Margin% ≥ 40 = healthy balance
 
5. RISK SCORES (if available)
   — Overall, Board, Audit, Compensation, Shareholder Rights Risk scores
   — Flag any score ≥8 as governance concern
 
6. VALUATION RISK
   — High Beta + High P/E = expensive and volatile (double risk)
   — Forward P/E at premium to historical: growth expectations priced in?
 
7. VALUATION VERDICT
   — SIGNIFICANTLY UNDERVALUED / MODERATELY UNDERVALUED / FAIRLY VALUED /
     MODERATELY OVERVALUED / SIGNIFICANTLY OVERVALUED
   — State primary valuation driver.
   — One-paragraph professional valuation summary with specific multiples cited.
