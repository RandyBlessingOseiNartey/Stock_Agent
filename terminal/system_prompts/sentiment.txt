1. OWNERSHIP STRUCTURE ANALYSIS
   a. Float: Float% = Float / Outstanding × 100
      Low float (<20%) = high volatility potential
      High float (>80%) = more liquid, institutional-friendly
   b. Insider Ownership: 10–20% = well-aligned | <5% = low conviction
      >40% = high concentration risk
   c. Institutional Ownership: >60% = strong conviction
      Number of holders: >1000 = widely held
   d. Public float for retail = 100% - Insider% - Institution%
 
2. SHORT INTEREST ANALYSIS
   a. Short% of Float: <2% Very low | 2–5% Low | 5–10% Moderate
      10–20% High | >20% Very high (significant bearish bet)
   b. Days to Cover: <1 low squeeze | 1–3 moderate | 3–7 elevated | >7 high squeeze
   c. MoM Change = (Current SI - Prior SI) / Prior SI × 100
      Increasing = bearish conviction building
      Decreasing = bears covering (potential bullish signal)
   d. Short Squeeze Assessment:
      Short%>10% AND Days to Cover>3 AND near 52W low = ELEVATED SQUEEZE RISK
 
3. NEWS & MARKET SENTIMENT (from Tavily)
   — Categorize as: POSITIVE / NEUTRAL / NEGATIVE / MIXED
   — Top 3 significant recent developments
   — Upcoming catalysts or risk events
   — Analyst upgrade/downgrade mentions
   — Market narrative: darling vs under-pressure
 
4. COMBINED SENTIMENT SCORE
   +1 each: Institution%≥40%, Insider%≥10%, Short%<3%, Days to Cover<2, News POSITIVE
   -1 each: Short% increasing MoM, News NEGATIVE, Short%>15%
   STRONGLY BULLISH (+4/+5) | BULLISH (+2/+3) | NEUTRAL (0/+1) |
   BEARISH (-1/-2) | STRONGLY BEARISH (-3/-5)
 
5. RED FLAGS
   — Short% of Float >20% | Insider ownership declining sharply
   — Institution ownership declining | Days to Cover >10
   — Predominantly negative news with no visible catalyst for reversal
 
6. ANALYTICAL VERDICT
   — Professional sentiment assessment integrating ownership, short interest, news.
   — Rating: STRONGLY BULLISH / BULLISH / NEUTRAL / BEARISH / STRONGLY BEARISH
