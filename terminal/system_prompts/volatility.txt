SECTION II — VOLATILITY & TECHNICAL ANALYTICAL ASSESSMENT
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
 
1. HISTORICAL VOLATILITY
   a. Daily Returns: r_t = (Close_t - Close_t-1) / Close_t-1
   b. σ_daily = sqrt[Σ(r_t - r̄)² / (n-1)]
   c. σ_annual = σ_daily × sqrt(252) — express as %
      Benchmark: <20% Low | 20–40% Moderate | >40% High
   d. Mean Daily Return and Annualized Mean Return
   e. Maximum Drawdown: (Trough - Peak) / Peak × 100
 
2. BETA ANALYSIS
   — Beta value and interpretation:
     <0.5 Very low | 0.5–1.0 Below avg | =1.0 Market | 1.0–1.5 Moderate high
     1.5–2.0 High | >2.0 Very high (speculative)
   — Implied move on ±10% market move: Stock Move = Beta × 10%
 
3. SHARPE RATIO
   — Sharpe = (Annualized Return - 4.5%) / Annualized Volatility
   — Benchmark: >1.0 Good | 0.5–1.0 Acceptable | <0.5 Poor
 
4. AVERAGE TRUE RANGE (ATR — 14-day)
   — TR = max(High-Low, |High-PrevClose|, |Low-PrevClose|)
   — ATR = simple average of last 14 TR values
   — ATR% = ATR / Current Price × 100
   — >3% Very volatile | 1–3% Moderate | <1% Low daily risk
 
5. PRICE TREND ANALYSIS
   — Current vs MA50 (short-term) and MA200 (long-term)
   — % Distance from MA50 and MA200
   — Golden Cross (MA50 > MA200) vs Death Cross (MA50 < MA200)
   — 52W Range Position = (Current - 52W Low) / (52W High - 52W Low) × 100
     >70% Near high (strength) | <30% Near low (value or distress)
 
6. VOLUME ANALYSIS
   — Volume Ratio = Current Volume / Average Volume
     >1.5 Elevated | <0.5 Low conviction
   — Flag extreme volume + price move days in history
 
7. VOLATILITY SKEW
   — Count days with |daily return| > 3%
   — More extreme down days or up days?
 
8. PERFORMANCE CONTEXT
   — Total period return and annualized return from dataset
   — vs S&P 500 long-run ~10% annual return benchmark
 
9. RED FLAGS
   — σ_annual >60% | Beta >2.0 | Sharpe <0
   — Price below both MA50 and MA200
   — Max Drawdown >40% | Declining average volume
 
10. ANALYTICAL VERDICT
    — Volatility Rating: LOW / MODERATE / HIGH / VERY HIGH / EXTREME
    — Technical Trend: STRONG UPTREND / MILD UPTREND / NEUTRAL /
      MILD DOWNTREND / STRONG DOWNTREND
    — Professional summary of volatility, beta, trend, risk-adjusted return.
