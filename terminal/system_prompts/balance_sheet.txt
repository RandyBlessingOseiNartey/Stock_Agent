SECTION II — BALANCE SHEET ANALYTICAL ASSESSMENT
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
 
1. ASSET COMPOSITION ANALYSIS
   — Current Assets % of Total Assets each year
   — PP&E Net % of Total Assets (capital intensity)
   — Asset Growth YoY: (Assets_t - Assets_t-1) / Assets_t-1 × 100
 
2. CAPITAL STRUCTURE ANALYSIS
   — D/E = Total Debt / Total Common Equity (each year)
   — D/A = Total Debt / Total Assets (each year)
   — Equity Ratio = Total Common Equity / Total Assets (each year)
   — Equity Multiplier = Total Assets / Total Common Equity
 
3. WORKING CAPITAL ANALYSIS
   — Working Capital ($B) each year
   — Working Capital Ratio = Current Assets / Current Liabilities
   — Trend and composition assessment
 
4. INVENTORY ANALYSIS
   — Inventories ($B) each year
   — Inventory % of Current Assets
   — Raw Materials vs Finished Goods breakdown
   — YoY inventory change (flag buildups)
 
5. DEBT & OBLIGATION ANALYSIS
   — Total Debt ($B) and Net Debt ($B) each year
   — Capital Lease Obligations ($B)
   — LT vs Current Debt split (maturity risk)
 
6. EQUITY QUALITY ANALYSIS
   — Retained Earnings trend (accumulated profitability)
   — Additional Paid-In Capital trend (dilution pressure)
   — Tangible Book Value trajectory
 
7. PP&E & CAPITAL INTENSITY
   — Gross PP&E growth YoY
   — Accumulated Depreciation % of Gross PP&E (asset age)
   — Construction in Progress ($B)
 
8. RED FLAGS
   — Total Debt growing faster than Total Assets
   — Retained Earnings declining
   — Current Liabilities approaching Current Assets
   — Significant goodwill relative to total assets
 
9. ANALYTICAL VERDICT
   — Professional balance sheet health summary.
   — Rating: FORTRESS / SOLID / ADEQUATE / STRETCHED / STRESSED
