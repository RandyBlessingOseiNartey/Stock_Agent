1. LIQUIDITY RATIOS (all years vs benchmarks)
   a. Current Ratio = Current Assets / Current Liabilities
      Benchmark: ≥2.0 Strong | 1.0–2.0 Adequate | <1.0 Distressed
   b. Quick Ratio = (Current Assets - Inventories) / Current Liabilities
      Benchmark: ≥1.0 Adequate | <1.0 Potential stress
   c. Cash Ratio = Total Cash & STI / Current Liabilities
      Benchmark: ≥0.5 Strong | <0.2 Low immediate liquidity
   d. OCF Ratio = Operating CF / Current Liabilities (if context available)
      Benchmark: ≥0.4 Healthy
 
2. WORKING CAPITAL ANALYSIS
   — Working Capital ($B) trend and YoY change
   — Decompose: what drives the change? (Receivables? Inventory? Payables?)
 
3. CASH RESERVE ANALYSIS
   — Total Cash & STI ($B) size and trend
   — Cash % of Total Current Liabilities
   — Cash % of Total Assets
 
4. CURRENT LIABILITIES COMPOSITION
   — AP % of Current Liabilities (trade credit usage)
   — Current Debt % of Current Liabilities (near-term debt pressure)
   — Deferred Revenue % (not a cash drain — favorable)
 
5. INVENTORY LIQUIDITY RISK
   — Inventories % of Current Assets
   — Raw Materials vs Finished Goods (finished = more liquid)
   — Flag if inventories >25% of current assets and growing
 
6. ACCOUNTS RECEIVABLE QUALITY
   — Receivables % of Current Assets
   — Flag if receivables growing faster than revenue
 
7. YoY LIQUIDITY TREND
   — Current and Quick Ratio trends across all 4 years
   — Flag any year with ratio deterioration >0.3
 
8. DEFERRED REVENUE CONSIDERATION
   — Note if significant portion of current liabilities is deferred revenue
     (service/product delivery obligation, not a cash outflow)
 
9. RED FLAGS
   — Current Ratio <1.0 | Quick Ratio <0.5
   — Current Debt > Cash | Declining Working Capital 3+ years
   — Receivables growing faster than revenue
 
10. ANALYTICAL VERDICT
    — Professional liquidity assessment.
    — Rating: VERY STRONG / STRONG / ADEQUATE / TIGHT / STRESSED
