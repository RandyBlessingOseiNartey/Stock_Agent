1. LEVERAGE RATIOS (each year vs benchmarks)
   a. D/E = Total Debt / Total Common Equity
      Benchmark: <1.0 Conservative | 1–2 Moderate | >2 Aggressive
   b. D/A = Total Debt / Total Assets
      Benchmark: <0.3 Low | 0.3–0.6 Moderate | >0.6 High
   c. Equity Ratio = Total Common Equity / Total Assets
   d. Net Debt ($B) = Total Debt - Cash & Equivalents
   e. Net Debt / EBITDA
      Benchmark: <1.0x Minimal | 1–3x Manageable | 3–5x Elevated | >5x Highly leveraged
   f. Equity Multiplier = Total Assets / Total Common Equity
 
2. DEBT COVERAGE RATIOS
   a. Interest Coverage = EBIT / Interest Expense
      Benchmark: >5x Very comfortable | 3–5x Adequate | <1.5x Distress
   b. EBITDA Coverage = EBITDA / Interest Expense
      Benchmark: >8x Strong | 4–8x Adequate | <3x Concern
   c. Debt Service Coverage = Operating Income / Interest Expense
 
3. CAPITAL STRUCTURE ANALYSIS
   — LT Debt % of Total Capitalization
   — Equity % of Total Capitalization
   — Capital lease obligations as off-balance-sheet debt proxy
 
4. DEBT MATURITY PROFILE
   — Current Debt vs LT Debt split
   — Current Debt % of Total Debt (refinancing risk)
   — Flag if >30% of total debt matures within the year
 
5. LEVERAGE TREND ANALYSIS
   — D/E, Net Debt/EBITDA, Interest Coverage trends across 4 years
   — Deleveraging or re-leveraging?
 
6. TAX SHIELD ANALYSIS
   — Tax Shield = Interest Expense × Effective Tax Rate (annual)
   — Quantify annual benefit from debt financing
 
7. RED FLAGS
   — Net Debt/EBITDA >4.0x | Interest Coverage <2.0x
   — D/E >3.0x without commensurate cash generation
   — Leverage increasing with declining EBITDA
   — >30% of debt maturing within 12 months with limited cash
 
8. ANALYTICAL VERDICT
   — Professional solvency assessment.
   — Rating: VERY CONSERVATIVE / CONSERVATIVE / MODERATE / ELEVATED / HIGHLY LEVERAGED
