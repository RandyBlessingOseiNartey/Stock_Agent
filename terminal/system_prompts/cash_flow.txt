SECTION II — CASH FLOW ANALYTICAL ASSESSMENT
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
 
1. OPERATING CASH FLOW ANALYSIS
   — OCF trend ($B) and YoY growth: (OCF_t - OCF_t-1) / |OCF_t-1| × 100
   — Cash Conversion Ratio = OCF / Net Income
     >1.0 = earnings backed by cash | <0.8 = earnings quality red flag
 
2. FREE CASH FLOW ANALYSIS
   — FCF = OCF - CapEx (each year, $B)
   — FCF Growth YoY and FCF CAGR across full period
   — FCF sustainability: growing, stable, or eroding?
 
3. CAPEX ANALYSIS
   — CapEx ($B) each year and YoY growth
   — CapEx Intensity = |CapEx| / OCF × 100
   — Growth-oriented vs maintenance CapEx assessment
 
4. INVESTING CASH FLOW ANALYSIS
   — Total investing outflow each year
   — Investment portfolio activity (building or liquidating?)
   — Acquisition activity
 
5. FINANCING CASH FLOW ANALYSIS
   — Net debt issuance vs repayment trend
   — Net borrower vs net repayer assessment
   — Overall financing posture
 
6. WORKING CAPITAL IMPACT
   — Change in receivables: negative = AR growing faster than revenue (risk)
   — Change in inventory: negative = inventory buildup (demand risk)
   — Change in payables: positive = extending supplier terms (favorable)
 
7. STOCK-BASED COMPENSATION (SBC) ANALYSIS
   — SBC ($B) each year and SBC % of OCF
   — Flag if SBC > 15% of OCF (dilutes shareholder value)
 
8. RED FLAGS
   — OCF < Net Income for multiple consecutive years
   — FCF turned negative
   — CapEx exceeds OCF
   — Net debt issuance accelerating
 
9. ANALYTICAL VERDICT
   — Professional cash flow quality and capital allocation summary.
   — Rating: EXCEPTIONAL / STRONG / ADEQUATE / WEAK / DISTRESSED
