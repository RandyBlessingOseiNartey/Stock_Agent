SECTION II — GROWTH ANALYTICAL ASSESSMENT
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
 
1. REVENUE GROWTH ANALYSIS
   — YoY Growth: (Revenue_t - Revenue_t-1) / Revenue_t-1 × 100 (each year)
   — Revenue CAGR: (Revenue_latest / Revenue_earliest)^(1/(n-1)) - 1
   — Trend: Accelerating / Stable / Decelerating / Negative
   — Volatility: compute standard deviation of growth rates
 
2. EARNINGS GROWTH ANALYSIS
   — YoY Net Income growth and EPS growth (both Basic & Diluted)
   — EPS CAGR across full period
   — EPS growth vs Revenue growth:
     EPS > Revenue growth = operating leverage (positive)
     EPS < Revenue growth = margin compression (negative)
   — Normalized Income growth for clean earnings trend
 
3. MARGIN TRAJECTORY ANALYSIS
   — Gross, Operating, Net Margin % each year
   — Margin change in bps: (Latest Margin - Earliest Margin) × 100
   — Operating Leverage = % Change in Op Income / % Change in Revenue
     >1.5x = strong operating leverage
 
4. R&D INVESTMENT
   — R&D % of Revenue and % of Gross Profit each year
   — R&D growth YoY
   — Increasing R&D = investing in future (positive)
   — Declining R&D in tech = potentially under-investing (red flag)
 
5. GROWTH QUALITY ASSESSMENT
   — Normalized vs Reported growth divergence (flag if >15%)
   — Consistency: how many of 4 years showed positive revenue growth?
   — Rule of 40: Revenue Growth% + EBITDA Margin% ≥ 40 = healthy
 
6. GROWTH SCORE (0–100)
   — Revenue CAGR:      ≥20%=25 | 10–20%=20 | 5–10%=12 | <5%=5
   — EPS CAGR:          ≥20%=25 | 10–20%=18 | 5–10%=10 | <5%=4
   — Margin Expansion:  Expanding=20 | Stable=10 | Contracting=0
   — Consistency:       4/4=15 | 3/4=10 | 2/4=5 | 1/4=0
   — Rule of 40:        ≥40=15 | 30–40=10 | <30=0
   — 80–100=High | 60–79=Solid | 40–59=Moderate | 20–39=Low | 0–19=Stalled
 
7. RED FLAGS
   — Revenue declining 3+ consecutive years
   — EPS growing despite declining revenue (cost-cutting, not real growth)
   — Gross margin declining >300bps annually
   — R&D cuts in innovation-dependent business
   — Normalized income consistently below reported income
 
8. ANALYTICAL VERDICT
   — Professional growth narrative with CAGRs, margin trajectory, sustainability.
   — Rating: ACCELERATING / STEADY / MODERATING / STALLING / DECLINING
